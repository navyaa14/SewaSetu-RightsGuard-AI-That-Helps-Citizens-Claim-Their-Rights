How to apply

  1. Verify your mobile number with OTP.
  2. Fill the application form (personal details, address, bank details).
  3. Upload your age proof document (JPG or PDF, maximum 5 MB).
  4. Submit the declaration. Note down your Application Number.

For assistance visit your nearest Block Development Office.
