Sewa Setu assistant: evaluation dashboard

Invented citizens and officers talking to a real MCP client, then checked against this portal's database.

Each person below is invented to stand for a way the counter goes wrong. In every run, a small model (Claude Code, headless, with a turn limit) is connected to our MCP endpoint and signed in through the portal's own OAuth flow, with the OTP read from the SMS gateway. A second model plays the person from the card. Afterwards we read the portal's database: the pass/fail below comes from what is actually stored, not from what the assistant said.
